## Telemetry vs Enforcement Policy

1. **`ENFORCING`**: Active in automated test/CI gates today; a regression fails the gate.
2. **`PENDING(Phase N)`**: Baseline and/or library-tier harness exists; full end-to-end enforcement lands at the exit of Bridge Phase `N` (`RABS_BRIDGE_PLAN.md`).
3. **Served-fraction telemetry (`bd-cl8mf`)**: Computed deterministically in permille (`rustc_served_permille` over cacheable `rustc` compile classes, with `LONG_RUN_SERVED_GOAL_PERMILLE = 900`) from durable `DecisionReceipt` records (`rabs_protocol::dashboards::served_fraction_from_receipts`). Its standing is `ServedFractionStanding::ReportedNotGated` until the Phase 5 / `M16` evidence-gated frontier review.
